Wilderness: The Exodus Protocol

Test prototype · isometric. You are Moses. Keep the people fed, watered and faithful for one week (Day 1–7).

• Tap the ground to walk (or WASD). Gather glowing Manna from dawn until 10:00, before it melts.
• Find rocks that shimmer, then Strike them with the staff for water.
• At dusk everyone eats and drinks. Shortfalls crush Faith.
• Open Overview to see traits, then tap a citizen to counsel them. Greedy hoarders rot manna; murmurers spread unrest.
• Day 6 gives double Manna. Save it for the Sabbath (Day 7: none falls).
• Pray near the pillar to restore Faith. At 0% they build the Golden Calf.
• Chain Manna pickups for a streak bonus.
• From Day 2, Amalekite raiders strike at night and steal Manna. Tap them to chase them down (devout citizens help), or sound the Shofar to rally everyone, speeding up the whole camp for 8s.
• Tap trees for wood, then press Build and tap near camp: Watchtowers (6 wood, max 4) stop raiders in range; the Tabernacle (20 wood) gives Faith +5 and steady recovery.
• Some springs run bitter: strike beside them again with 2 wood to sweeten them. On Days 3 and 5, quail fly in at noon. Grab them before nightfall.
