          The Journey

          Follow the Pillar

          Follow the Pillar. A pillar of cloud by day and fire by night stands over the next station. The compass points to it. Reach the station to hear its story and receive its blessing — then the pillar moves on.

          Survive. Hunger, water, warmth, stamina and faith all matter. Manna feeds you and the camp; the waterskin carries water; the campfire restores warmth; the Sabbath restores faith.

          Gather. Touch the glowing manna flakes. The camp quota is eaten at dusk. Surplus can be sold at the market, and silver can buy water or be tithed.

          The Camp. The people need food, water and leadership. Each month brings a reckoning; shortages cost faith and lives. Reach all eight stations before the fortieth year ends.
